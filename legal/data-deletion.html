Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated:

You can delete the data InstaPulse holds about you at any time.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. In InstaPulse, open the sidebar and click ✕ next to each connected account. This deletes its data stored in your browser.
  2. In Planning, remove (✕) any scheduled posts. Cancelled posts are not published.
  3. Remove InstaPulse's access on each platform:
    • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → InstaPulse → Remove
    • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → InstaPulse → Remove
    • Google/YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions → InstaPulse → Remove access
    • LinkedIn: Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services; TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions

Option 2 — Ask us to delete it

E-mail with the subject "Data deletion request" and your Instagram/Facebook username. We will delete all scheduled-post records and stored tokens linked to your accounts within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

Access tokens, scheduled and published post records, and uploaded media held by InstaPulse. Content already published to your social accounts stays there; delete it on that platform if you wish.
