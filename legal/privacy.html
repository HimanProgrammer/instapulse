Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This policy explains how ("we") collects, uses and protects information when you use InstaPulse (instapulse-lime.vercel.app), a social media analytics, scheduling and inbox tool.

1. Information we collect

  • Account connections. When you log in with Instagram, Facebook, YouTube (Google), LinkedIn or TikTok, we receive an access token and basic profile information (user ID, username, display name) for the accounts you choose to connect.
  • Content and insights. Using that token, InstaPulse reads your posts, follower and reach insights, comments and direct messages so we can show them to you, and publishes the posts, replies and messages you create.
  • Scheduled posts. Posts you schedule (media URL, caption, time, post type) are stored on our server until they are published.
  • Media you upload for publishing, if you use the upload feature.
  • Data stored in your browser. Dashboard data, settings and your optional Anthropic API key are kept in your browser's local storage and are not sent to our servers.

2. How we use information

Only to provide the features you use: show analytics, publish and schedule your posts, display and reply to comments and messages, research hashtags and public competitor accounts, and generate AI suggestions when you ask. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except as described below.

3. Third-party services

  • Meta (Instagram, Facebook), Google (YouTube), LinkedIn and TikTok — to read and publish content on the accounts you connect, under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — when you use AI features, the relevant dashboard data, captions, comments or screenshots are sent to Anthropic's API using your key to generate a reply.
  • Vercel (hosting) and Supabase (database for scheduled posts, servers in India).

4. Storage and security

Access tokens for scheduled posts are stored in a database that is not publicly accessible and is used only by our server to publish your posts. All traffic uses HTTPS. Instagram and Facebook tokens expire after about 60 days.

5. Retention

Scheduled-post records are kept until you delete them or request deletion. Data in your browser stays until you clear it or remove the account in InstaPulse.

6. Your rights and choices

You can disconnect any account at any time, remove InstaPulse's access from the platform's settings, and request access to or deletion of your data. See our Data Deletion instructions.

7. Children

InstaPulse is not intended for anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
